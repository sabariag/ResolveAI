RESOLVEAI CUSTOMER RESOLUTION POLICIES

POLICY: DAMAGED PRODUCT REPLACEMENT
Customers may request a replacement when a delivered product is damaged.
The order must be verified before a replacement is created.
A replacement can only be created when replacement inventory is available.
If the preferred warehouse has no inventory, another warehouse with available inventory may be selected.

POLICY: REFUND FOR DAMAGED PRODUCT
A customer may receive a refund for a damaged product when the order is eligible for a refund.
The order must be verified before issuing the refund.
The refund should use the original payment method when supported.

POLICY: ORDER CANCELLATION
An order may be cancelled when it has not entered the non-cancellable fulfillment stage.
Orders that have already reached a non-cancellable stage cannot be cancelled through the normal cancellation process.

POLICY: REPLACEMENT INVENTORY
A replacement requires at least one available unit of the requested product.
The agent should check inventory before creating a replacement.
If one warehouse is unavailable, the agent may use another warehouse with available inventory.

POLICY: CUSTOMER VERIFICATION
Before performing a replacement, refund, or cancellation, the customer and order must be verified.
The resolution agent should not execute an action when the required customer or order information cannot be verified.

POLICY: ESCALATION
The issue should be escalated when the required policy conditions cannot be satisfied or when the required enterprise-system information cannot be verified.
